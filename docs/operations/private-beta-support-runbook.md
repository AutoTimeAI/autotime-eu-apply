# Private-beta support runbook

Status: channel delivery BLOCKED until founder configures and tests it.

1. Publish a monitored support address/form and named primary/backup owner.
2. Test with a synthetic message; retain redacted receipt, acknowledgement timestamp and ticket/reference.
3. Triage: P0 account/data/security within 15 minutes; P1 core workflow within 4 hours; P2 within one business day.
4. Never request passwords, tokens, full CVs or unnecessary personal data. Redact attachments before engineering escalation.
5. For suspected security/privacy events, transfer to the incident plan and preserve an audit trail.
6. Invitations remain disabled whenever a P0 is open or a mandatory gate regresses.

Required evidence: channel identifier (not credentials), ownership, test timestamp, response time, retention policy and tested SHA.
