# AutoTime competitive feature audit — verified 18 August 2026

## Executive conclusion

The supplied HTML audit identifies a real distribution disadvantage, but it understates the current product and overstates several uniqueness claims. It should not be used externally in its current form.

AutoTime's defensible position is **evidence-led, consent-first cross-border application support for Europe**. It is not “the only product” with mobility information, interview practice, or outreach tracking. The differentiation is the combination of role-level work-permission reasoning, evidence-linked outputs, ESCO-based matching, and explicit human review.

## Corrections to the supplied audit

- The methodology says 12 competitors, but names 16 products/platforms.
- The scorecard contains 11 rows, while its totals account for only 10.
- “No competitor found” is not evidence of market uniqueness. Use “not found in the reviewed public product material as of 18 August 2026.”
- Bulk auto-apply is a deliberate non-goal and trust trade-off, not an implementation failure.
- AutoTime already has question-by-question interview practice, confidence capture, evidence-aware answer drafts, technical drills, and follow-up prompts. “Static prep only” is stale.
- AutoTime already has a human-sent outreach workspace with contact types, editable drafts, statuses, and follow-up dates. The remaining gap is **contact discovery/import**, not outreach tracking itself.
- Platform coverage must be split into three measures:
  1. browser capture recognition;
  2. tested extraction/autofill compatibility;
  3. server-side job-feed ingestion.
  Counting these as one number is misleading.

## Verified competitor evidence

- Simplify says Copilot works across 100+ job boards and ATSs and its help centre says it supports about 80% of job sites. Its Talent Agent also markets warm introductions and an early-access Autopilot.
- JobCopilot markets automated applications across more than 500,000 company career pages and hiring-manager email discovery.
- Careerflow markets an AI mock interview and a networking tracker/CRM.
- Teal documents a contact tracker, LinkedIn contact capture, and job tracking across 40+ boards.
- EURES says its jobs come from public employment services and partners in 31 countries; “EURES jobs” indicate employer interest in recruiting cross-border. It explicitly notes that non-EU work/residence requirements still apply.

These are vendors' current public claims, not independent success-rate measurements.

## Priorities

1. **Measure real coverage.** Publish tested browser-capture and autofill coverage separately from URL recognition and feed ingestion.
2. **Expand native feeds carefully.** Add high-volume public ATS feeds with fixtures, rate controls and failure reporting. SmartRecruiters is the first addition from this review.
3. **Improve contact acquisition without scraping.** Add CSV/manual import and permitted data-provider integrations; retain human-sent messaging.
4. **Prove the differentiation.** Instrument the funnel from imported role → reviewed brief → evidence-backed application → interview, and measure completion and correction rates.
5. **Do not chase mass apply.** Compete on decision quality, mobility confidence and truthful applications.

## Release claim guardrails

Public copy may say:

> AutoTime combines evidence-backed application support with explainable European mobility guidance and human-reviewed workflows.

Public copy should not say “only,” “guaranteed,” “all ATSs,” or quote a compatibility count unless the count links to a dated test matrix.
