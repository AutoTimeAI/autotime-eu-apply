# Founder Decision Memo

## Company thesis

Fund AutoTime as a **candidate-controlled, evidence-backed cross-border application decision system**. Do not fund it as a broad AI career suite or a collection of visa calculators.

The product should help a non-EU technologist answer, before applying:

> Is this specific role with this specific employer realistically actionable for me under a current, source-linked route; what evidence controls that conclusion; what is uncertain; and what should be verified next?

This remains a hypothesis until users understand it, change decisions appropriately, return and pay.

## Evidence-backed strategic changes

1. **Germany remains first.** It accounted for 72% of EU Blue Cards in 2024 and has a large technology workforce plus structured 2026 thresholds.
2. **The Netherlands remains paired first.** Its recognised-sponsor register creates a valuable authoritative employer-verification primitive distinct from German route logic.
3. **Poland moves up.** Second-highest EU Blue Card issuance and a current digital MOS workflow justify accelerated research; the earlier watchlist treatment was too weakly evidenced.
4. **Blue Card availability is not demand.** Sweden, Estonia and Portugal issued fewer than 100 Blue Cards each in 2024; Denmark and Ireland do not participate and must be judged using national permits.
5. **Generic shortage messaging is unsafe.** EU tech employment has grown, but information-and-communication vacancy rates softened. Target corridor evidence must replace a Europe-wide shortage claim.
6. **Immigration screening is not empty whitespace.** Jobbatical already advertises instant/pre-hire assessments; Localyze and Deel combine software and experts. AutoTime must differentiate before the case begins and through candidate-owned evidence/replay.
7. **The moat is composite.** Public rules, LLMs, job tracking and basic calculators are copyable. The plausible durable asset is the linked history of sources, candidate evidence, employer identity, decisions, corrections and outcomes.

## Fund now

- Versioned source documents/spans and atomic rules.
- Candidate evidence provenance and confirmation.
- Immutable vacancy and decision snapshots.
- Deterministic rule compilation with unknown/conflict states.
- Netherlands sponsor entity resolution with precision-first verification.
- Decision replay and source dependency graph.
- Correction/disagreement and expert sign-off workflows.
- Germany/Netherlands golden and adversarial corpora.
- Founder-led concierge UAT and real checkout experiments.

## Defer

- More generic career-direction breadth.
- Broad job discovery and aggregator ambitions.
- Interview features unrelated to the evidence spine.
- Large-scale autofill compatibility work.
- Equal production launch across 15–20 countries.
- Employer candidate ranking/scoring.
- Outcome marketing such as “more interviews.”

## Partner

- Regulated immigration advice and application representation.
- Per-jurisdiction expert review.
- ESCO and official occupational taxonomies.
- Employer/company identifiers where licensing and provenance permit.
- Commodity model inference and generic document formatting.
- Potential referral fulfilment with employer-side mobility providers, provided candidate independence and data terms are acceptable.

## Stop conditions

Stop or materially narrow the thesis if, after a properly run Germany/Netherlands concierge beta:

- users primarily value resume writing or autofill;
- fewer than three of five target users pay for a concrete offer;
- repeat vacancy evaluation is below 50% of activated testers;
- users cannot understand conditional/unknown outputs;
- expert review frequently overturns apparently deterministic conclusions;
- sponsor entity matching cannot avoid harmful false verification;
- country operations and expert cost prevent a credible contribution margin;
- public/general-AI alternatives are perceived as equally trustworthy and actionable.

## 90-day programme

### Days 0–30 — reproducible truth spine

- Resolve German and Dutch controlling-source chains.
- Encode source, span, rule bundle, evidence, vacancy, employer and decision contracts.
- Create 50 cases per route across ordinary/boundary/adversarial classes.
- Implement exact sponsor lookup and ambiguity states.
- Produce UK written-scope brief; keep personalised UK outputs disabled.
- Recruit five target users and prepare real payment offers.

Exit: one German and one Dutch decision can be reproduced and explained entirely from stored evidence.

### Days 31–60 — replay, correction and UAT

- Run source-change simulation and impacted-decision replay.
- Add correction/disagreement and reviewer workflows.
- Conduct 50 founder-observed vacancy decisions.
- Measure comprehension, changed behaviour, repeat use and support cost.
- Run real Stripe checkout and refund flow.

Exit: 100% sampled replay, zero false sponsor verification, ≥80% comprehension and evidence of appropriate decision change.

### Days 61–90 — bounded private beta

- Obtain scoped Germany/Netherlands expert sign-off.
- Complete DPIA inputs, retention classes and deletion/recovery tests.
- Publish source/readiness status and incident behaviour.
- Test £19 decision pack, £29 monthly workspace and £99 reviewed pack.
- Decide fund/defer/stop using paid and operational evidence.

Exit: at least three real payers, repeat use by at least three of five testers, a contribution-margin path and no unresolved critical rule/source issue.

## Founder dashboard

Review weekly:

- source freshness and unresolved critical diffs;
- replay and citation completeness;
- false/ambiguous sponsor matches;
- expert disagreement and correction closure;
- user comprehension and appropriate decision changes;
- repeat vacancy evaluations;
- paid conversion/refunds;
- fully loaded contribution per route/country;
- percentage of work spent on the core evidence loop versus generic features.

## Final judgement

This can become a moat edge, but not because Europe has many rules. Complexity by itself is a cost. The moat forms only when AutoTime converts that complexity into trusted, reproducible candidate decisions and accumulates evidence competitors cannot instantly purchase: confirmed facts, corrected mappings, replay history, expert-labelled boundaries and longitudinal behaviour/outcomes.

The immediate company-building move is depth in two complementary markets, paid validation and operational proof—not breadth masquerading as coverage.
