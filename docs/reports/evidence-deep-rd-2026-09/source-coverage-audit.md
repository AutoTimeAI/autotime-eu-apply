# Source coverage and residual-risk audit

**Audit date:** 11 September 2026  
**Unit:** a third-country technology candidate assessing an employer-sponsored route.  
**Interpretation:** coverage means at least one traceable route claim; it does not mean a market is safe for automated conclusions.

## Coverage result

The claim-ledger count is calculated by the package validator and must not be treated as static prose. Every target market has route-level evidence, with Belgium represented as three legally material regional branches. Germany and the Netherlands have the strongest product-ready chains. Czechia and Lithuania now have current 2026 numeric chains and transition tests; Italy, Portugal, parts of Belgium and several underlying instruments still have unresolved dependencies. Switzerland remains inherently judgement-heavy and the United Kingdom remains output-restricted pending an IAA scope opinion.

## Source hierarchy

1. Legislation or official consolidated law.
2. Responsible ministry, immigration authority or regional authority.
3. European Commission portal where the national chain is incomplete.
4. Official statistics for variables used by a legal formula.
5. Commercial or secondary material only for competitor/product assertions, never as a mobility-rule authority.

## Known contradictions and freshness hazards

- Ireland publishes €40,904/€36,848 on the route page and €40,909/€36,849 in its remuneration table. S.I. 643/2025 resolves the controlling values at €40,904/€36,848; the inconsistent table remains a monitored source-quality defect rather than a decision blocker.
- Flanders explicitly says 2025 values remain in use while newer Statbel statistics are pending. This is a live change-detection test case.
- Italy and Portugal still lack a sufficiently resolved current numeric chain. Estonia has a derived numeric chain pending administrative confirmation; Czechia and Lithuania now have official current amounts and effective boundaries.
- UK sponsor status and Netherlands recognised-sponsor status are mutable registries. A match without retrieval time and entity identity is not durable evidence.
- Swiss admission depends on quota, canton, employer recruitment evidence and judgement; a deterministic eligibility label would overstate the evidence.

## Release rule

A market may move to user-facing decision output only when all critical claims have a current primary source, effective interval, parsed rule, boundary tests, independent reviewer, and rollback/replay procedure. Otherwise the product may show sourced general information and an evidence checklist, with the unresolved dependency named.
