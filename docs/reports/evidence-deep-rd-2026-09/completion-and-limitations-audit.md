# Phase 2 completion and limitations audit

**Audit date:** 12 September 2026  
**Desk-research judgement:** **Complete for startup investment, product architecture and controlled corridor selection.**  
**Startup/product validation judgement:** **Not complete and not represented as complete.**

## Completion definition

Phase 2 was commissioned to create an evidence-deep European R&D foundation: strengthen 20 jurisdiction dossiers; build primary-law and administrative source chains with temporal and supersession controls; test the moat against competitors and substitutes; improve market economics and sequencing; specify evaluation and governance; audit material claims; and separate desk findings from evidence that requires experts, users, transactions or production operation.

Completion does not mean every European route is safe to automate. A rigorous result may be `review_required`, `source_conflict`, `authority_review` or `regulated_review` when the evidence shows that deterministic output would be unsafe. The completion test is whether those limits are evidenced, traceable, tested and converted into product gates.

## Findings suitable for founder decisions

1. The investable wedge is candidate-controlled, vacancy-specific cross-border decision evidence—not generic resume writing, autofill, tracking or country information.
2. Rules, citations, experts, audit trails and broad jurisdiction coverage are not individually a moat. Employer-side immigration and legal incumbents already possess several at scale.
3. The potentially compounding asset is the consented relationship among candidate evidence, vacancy/employer identity, effective rules, decision, correction, action and verified outcome.
4. Germany and the Netherlands remain the preferred validation corridor, but for different reasons: German revealed Blue Card volume and Dutch sponsor verifiability. CBS data disproves a Netherlands growth narrative.
5. Poland merits the next research/validation wave because revealed Blue Card activity is materially larger than most nominal alternatives.
6. Twenty-market production breadth is not justified. Each jurisdiction must earn release independently through source completeness, tests, expert approval, user value and contribution margin.

## Desk-research limitations converted into product states

The package deliberately does not manufacture certainty where controlling evidence remains inaccessible, inconsistent or job-specific:

- France salary remains `source_conflict` because current official values disagree, while duration is resolved at six months.
- Portugal remains `review_required` for the Blue Card statistic and Tech Visa rounding; employer certification and responsibility-term provenance are now captured.
- Italy remains `review_required` because the exact ISTAT observation and applicable job/employer CCNL cannot be reduced to one national number.
- Belgium requires region determination, Brussels canonical confirmation and the Flanders successor statistic.
- Luxembourg's administrative EUR 65,652 is not promoted to statutory provenance without the controlling amount instrument.
- Estonia's derived amount remains reviewable pending administrative confirmation of series and rounding.
- Austria can evaluate candidate predicates but cannot infer the AMS labour-market outcome.
- Switzerland is `authority_review`; the United Kingdom is `regulated_review` pending a written IAA scope opinion.

These are completed research conclusions and release restrictions, not hidden omissions.

## Evidence that only external work can produce

The following programme remains open after Phase 2 desk research:

- qualified jurisdiction-expert signatures and marketed-language approval;
- founder-led UAT with at least 3–5 target users, then a larger directional cohort;
- observed comprehension, trust, disagreement and appropriate apply/skip changes;
- paid Stripe transactions, refunds, repeat use and fully loaded contribution margin;
- real Resend delivery, Sentry events/alerts and installed-extension/Chrome Web Store validation;
- production source-change detection, incident response, replay and correction records;
- verified retention enforcement, deletion cascades, backups and recovery drills;
- causal outcome evidence before any “more interviews” claim.

No desk document, including this one, substitutes for those artefacts.

## Final conclusion

Phase 2 evidence-deep **desk R&D is complete** as a startup decision and engineering foundation. It establishes what to build, what may be defensible, where to begin, what not to claim, how evidence must be governed, and exactly where the product must refuse certainty.

The wider startup validation programme remains open. Production or market launch should advance only through the corridor, expert, user, commercial and operational gates documented in this package.
