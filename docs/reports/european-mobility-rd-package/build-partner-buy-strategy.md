# Build, Partner and Buy Strategy

## Principle

Build only the assets whose accumulation could strengthen AutoTime's decision trust or economics.
Buy commodity infrastructure. Partner where professional authority, proprietary access or
distribution is more defensible than ownership.

## Decision matrix

| Capability                           | Default                                                  | Rationale                                                         | Revisit trigger                                                                |
| ------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Rule/source version graph            | Build                                                    | Core lineage, replay and correction asset                         | A provider offers exportable exact revisions/rules with durable rights         |
| Deterministic evaluator/trace        | Build                                                    | Product-specific safety and inspectability                        | None without preserving full audit control                                     |

| Employer entity/assertion graph      | Build thin core; license authoritative data where needed | Matching/history may compound; raw registries are not proprietary | Reliable licensed entity-resolution service beats internal precision/economics |
| Legal/immigration interpretation     | Partner                                                  | Requires jurisdiction competence and accountability               | In-house regulated function justified by scale/cost                            |
| Expert reviewer network              | Partner first                                            | Speed, authority and route-specific depth                         | Volume supports dedicated staff and authorisation                              |
| Visa/application filing              | Partner or out of scope                                  | Different regulated operational business                          | Paid cohorts demand it and partner economics/quality are proven                |
| LLM generation/extraction            | Buy through replaceable interface                        | Models are commodity and fast-changing                            | Specific model produces defensible evaluated performance/cost                  |

## Partnership hypotheses

### Regulated mobility advisers

Offer structured pre-application packets and reviewer tooling. AutoTime supplies evidence lineage and
triage; the partner supplies authorised judgement/individual service. Test review-time reduction,
correction rate, response SLO, customer ownership and margin before claiming a network moat.

### Relocation job marketplaces

Relocate.me/VanHack-like channels may own candidate intent and sponsored-job discovery. A partnership
could attach evidence-backed vacancy decisions to curated jobs. Test incremental activation and paid
conversion; avoid giving away the evidence graph or becoming an undifferentiated checker.

### Employer mobility platforms

Deel/Jobbatical/Localyze operate later in the lifecycle. A referral/export partnership could move a
candidate's evidence packet into post-offer processing. It is attractive if AutoTime retains
candidate trust/data control and receives outcome signals lawfully. It is dangerous if the incumbent
can absorb the pre-application feature and distribution.

### Coaches, communities and universities

Useful for distribution and comprehension studies. Ensure incentives do not turn coached or
subsidised use into false willingness-to-pay evidence.

## Partner diligence

For each partner record authority/qualifications, scope/countries/routes, ownership and portability of
data/labels, subprocessor/transfers, security, insurance, SLA, incident cooperation, source-update
method, correction/appeal, customer relationship, pricing/margin, termination/export, exclusivity and
competitive use rights.

## Moat leakage controls

- Never grant a partner unrestricted rights to the aggregated rule, correction or labelled boundary
  corpus without explicit strategic value.
- Preserve candidate consent and portability; do not use partner access to broaden purposes silently.
- Require exportable structured review labels and exact version binding.
- Avoid exclusive distribution until channel economics and dependence are measured.
- Maintain replaceable interfaces for models and commodity vendors.

## Decision tests

Build when the capability is core to trust, improves through proprietary use and can be operated
reliably. Partner when authority/distribution/data access dominates and incentives can align. Buy when
the capability is commodity, replaceable and not the source of differentiation. Remove when none of
the three paths supports measurable customer value or safety.
