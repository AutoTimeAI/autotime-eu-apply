# 90-Day Founder Operating Plan

## Outcome

At day 90, decide to continue the candidate decision product, narrow it to an evidence/expert
workflow, partner, or stop. Shipping more features is not the outcome.

## Days 1–15 — scope and authority

- Adopt DE/NL, target persona, output taxonomy and prohibited claims.
- Recruit eight or more discovery participants and qualified DE/NL reviewer candidates.
- Brief counsel/DPO; start DPIA and advice-boundary opinions.
- Define one paid Decision Pack and acquisition channel.
- Complete MOB-001/MOB-002 live-path map and canonical-contract ADR.
- Convert the 40-case CSV into fixtures without inventing expert labels.

Checkpoint evidence: signed scope, recruitment log, legal/DPIA briefs, reachability report, fixture
parser and offer version.

## Days 16–30 — governed shadow foundation

- Build immutable source revisions, claims/links and constrained rule bundles.
- Encode DE/NL drafts from exact source revisions.
- Import two IND register snapshots or a controlled revision fixture.
- Run discovery sessions and publish denominators/adverse findings.
- Complete expert rule review and isolate blind holdout cases.
- Simulate a critical source diff.

Stop broad product work if the problem is not repeated/specific. Narrow to an expert evidence
workspace if ambiguity prevents safe automation.

## Days 31–45 — decision/replay and trust prototype

- Shadow-evaluate route status separately from product action.
- Persist immutable traces; prove old/current replay.
- Implement precision-first entity states and claim gate.
- Prototype evidence/reason/limit UX without optimising visual confidence.
- Resolve all internal critical/high corpus failures.

Checkpoint evidence: version manifest, test and entity reports, replay and containment drill.

## Days 46–60 — external comprehension and production controls

- Run 12–20 randomised comprehension/reliance sessions with flawed cases.
- Obtain exact-version expert sign-off or remain research-only.
- Complete DPIA/counsel decisions and approved language.
- Verify Stripe, Resend, Sentry, extension, retention/deletion and recovery.

Pause on systematic over-reliance, unresolved authority, critical errors or failed controls.

## Days 61–75 — paid private cohort

- Expose the frozen Decision Pack to qualified prospects.
- Record funnel, real Stripe events and all delivery costs.
- Deliver within signed scope; record corrections/support time.
- Compare a subset with official checklist, general LLM and accessible competitors.

Do not change offer/price/qualification mid-cohort without a new cell/deviation.

## Days 76–90 — repeatability decision

- Seek repeat decisions and second payments without invalidating founder prompting.
- Reconcile contribution with expert/source/support/correction cost.
- Analyse trust, behaviour, errors and demand by route/channel/offer.
- Run another source-change/replay/notification drill.
- Publish go/narrow/partner/stop memo including negative evidence and runway impact.

## Weekly control meeting

Review release modes, critical/high errors, source/sign-off freshness, user reliance, funnel/retention,
delivery contribution, regulatory/production blockers and decisions. Exclude country and feature count.

## Day-90 choices

- **Continue:** safety/trust pass, paid repeat signal and credible cost trend.
- **Narrow:** evidence is valued but automated decisions/subscription fail.
- **Partner:** authority/distribution requires a stronger partner model.
- **Stop:** unsafe performance, no paid repeat, or substitutes match value.
- **Expand one market:** only after DE/NL proof and evidence-based selection.

Maintain scope decisions, raw sessions, sign-offs, manifests, evaluation, outputs, production proof,
Stripe reconciliation, costs, incidents, DPIA/opinions and final decision in the evidence room.
