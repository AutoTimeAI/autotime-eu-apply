# Competitive and Substitute Landscape

**As-of date:** 10 September 2026  
**Status:** desk-research baseline; public claims are not independent performance validation.

## Competitive conclusion

AutoTime does not occupy an empty category. It sits between three established groups:

1. **Job-search productivity suites** make applications faster and documents more keyword-aligned.
2. **Employer mobility platforms and immigration firms** combine case software, maintained
   jurisdiction knowledge and human expertise.
3. **Relocation-focused tech marketplaces** pre-filter or curate sponsor/relocation vacancies and
   can remove the need to assess unsuitable jobs at all.
4. **Free official and public infrastructure** supplies authoritative rules, sponsor registers,
   occupations and vacancies.

The defensible position is narrower: **candidate-side, pre-application, evidence-backed cross-border
decisioning that remains reproducible as rules change and carries verified facts into the application.**
Neither “AI resume writer” nor “visa checker” is sufficient differentiation.

## Free substitutes

| Substitute                           | Why users may prefer it                                | AutoTime must beat it on                                                                   |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| National immigration authority pages | Free and authoritative                                 | Route discovery, current-source synthesis, personal evidence gaps and replay               |
| EU Immigration Portal                | Free cross-EU orientation                              | National implementation detail and application-specific evidence                           |
| IND/UK sponsor registers             | Authoritative employer status                          | Legal-entity resolution, vacancy linkage, history and understandable uncertainty           |
| EURES                                | Free vacancies and cross-border employment information | Non-EU route feasibility and evidence quality for a specific vacancy                       |
| ESCO                                 | Multilingual occupation/skills identifiers             | Candidate/job evidence mapping and country-rule consequences                               |
| Search engines and general LLMs      | Immediate and familiar                                 | Citability, effective dates, abstention, correction and reproducibility                    |
| Immigration lawyers/advisers         | Professional accountability and case judgement         | Earlier screening, lower price, repeatability and clean escalation packets—not replacement |
| Spreadsheets/checklists              | Transparent and controllable                           | Automation without losing inspectability or ownership                                      |

## Feature parity versus moat

| Capability         | Commodity/parity          | Potentially defensible only when…                                                 |
| ------------------ | ------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Resume generation  | Yes                       | Claims are evidence-bound and unsupported text is blocked                         |
| Browser autofill   | Yes                       | Field values retain provenance and risky answers require approval                 |
| Job tracking       | Yes                       | It records decisions/corrections/outcomes needed for validated learning           |
| Visa checklist     | Mostly                    | It is route/version-specific, monitored and expert-signed                         |
| Eligibility score  | Easy to imitate           | Every result is replayable, calibrated and safely abstains                        |
| Sponsor badge      | Easy superficially        | Entity match, source snapshot, revocation and ambiguity are governed              |
| Country comparison | Easy superficially        | Same candidate/vacancy facts are evaluated consistently across versioned routes   |
| Human review       | Available from incumbents | Reviewer decisions become structured corrections without exposing privileged data |
| Outcome learning   | Potential moat            | Consent, selection bias and causal limitations are handled honestly               |

## Competitive tests—not assertions

The moat thesis passes only if AutoTime can demonstrate all of the following:

- Target users make more appropriate apply/skip/review decisions than with official links alone.
- Users can explain the decisive facts and uncertainty after seeing the result.
- A saved decision replays identically under its original rule bundle and produces an intelligible
  delta under a later bundle.
- Sponsor verification has near-zero false-positive “verified” labels.
- Users pay and return for multiple vacancies before an employer has opened an immigration case.
- Expert review cost declines per usable decision without degrading critical-error rates.
- Corrections and outcomes measurably improve rules, extraction or evidence prompts.

If those conditions fail, the rational product is a focused workflow utility or expert lead-
generation service—not a data/decision moat.

## Positioning recommendation

Avoid “AI job application platform,” “visa eligibility checker” and “get more interviews.” Use a
bounded promise:

> **Decide which European tech applications are worth pursuing—with current official rules,
> verified employer evidence and a record you can inspect.**

This positioning must remain a hypothesis until users understand it, pay for it and behave better
because of it.
