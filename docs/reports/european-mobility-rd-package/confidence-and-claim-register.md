## Confidence governance

- Confidence attaches to a claim version, not an entire report.
- Source authority, currency, completeness, consistency and directness are evaluated separately.
- High confidence in a risk does not equal legal permission to launch.
- Analytical estimates retain ranges and falsification evidence.
- New contradictory evidence reopens the claim and triggers dependent decision review.
