# Product, Trust and Business Validation Programme

**Status:** executable protocol; no sessions, payments or expert sign-offs are claimed complete.

## Research questions

1. Can target users correctly interpret a route recommendation and its uncertainty?
2. Does the product change apply/skip/review behaviour for defensible reasons?
3. Does it reduce time or errors relative to official sources and a clear checklist?
4. Will users pay before an employer has initiated an immigration case?
5. Do they return for multiple vacancies and keep evidence current?
6. Can experts review cases faster without increasing critical errors?
7. Do corrections and outcomes produce proprietary, usable learning?

## Study 1 — problem and language discovery

- **Participants:** 8–12 non-EU software/data/security/product professionals who applied or plan to
  apply to Germany/Netherlands within six months.
- **Sampling:** include degree/non-degree, early/senior career, visa-experienced/inexperienced and at
  least three origin regions. Exclude friends/family from the core evidence count.
- **Method:** recent-decision interview using an actual vacancy; no feature pitch for first half.
- **Evidence:** current workflow, sources used, mistakes, abandoned applications, trust cues, spend,
  and exact language used for uncertainty.
- **Pass:** at least 6/8 report a repeated pre-application decision problem and show real artefacts.
- **Fail:** pain is primarily resume writing, job discovery or post-offer legal processing.

## Study 2 — recommendation comprehension and appropriate reliance

- **Participants:** 12–20 target users not used to author the cases.
- **Materials:** matched scenarios with correct, incomplete, stale and deliberately flawed outputs;
  official-links checklist baseline; blinded presentation order.
- **Tasks:** state recommendation, decisive facts, uncertainty, missing evidence and next action.
- **Metrics:** comprehension accuracy; detection of flawed advice; over-reliance; under-reliance;
  time; confidence calibration; escalation choice.
- **Pass:** ≥85% correct comprehension, ≥80% detection of critical flawed outputs, and no critical
  scenario where a majority over-relies.
- **Rule:** a more confident UI that lowers error detection fails even if users prefer it.

## Study 3 — behavioural decision quality

- **Design:** each participant evaluates comparable real vacancies unaided, with official checklist,
  and with AutoTime. Expert-labelled cases provide a reference outcome.
- **Primary endpoint:** reduction in critical inappropriate apply/skip decisions.
- **Secondary:** time, missing-evidence discovery, employer-verification accuracy and decision change.
- **Bias controls:** randomise condition order; record prior route knowledge; separate title from full
  duties; do not count changes as improvements unless expert labels support them.
- **Pass:** meaningful error reduction versus both baselines without increased false-green decisions.

## Study 4 — paid concierge pilot

- **Offer:** five DE/NL vacancy decisions plus reusable evidence profile; manual research/expert steps
  are disclosed.
- **Minimum funnel:** 100 qualified landing/DM recipients → 30 serious conversations → 10 checkout
  attempts → 5 completed payments → 3 completed packs → 2 repeat purchases/renewals.
- **Record:** channel, message, eligibility for sample, price, payment, refund, delivery minutes,
  corrections, repeat decision and stated/observed alternative.
- **Pass:** three genuine payments and two repeat-use events with positive contribution trajectory.
- **Invalid evidence:** founder-funded payments, discounts not recorded, verbal willingness-to-pay,
  or a payment from someone outside the target segment.

## Study 5 — expert co-pilot evaluation

- **Participants:** at least two qualified reviewers per production route where feasible.
- **Comparison:** normal review versus structured AutoTime packet.
- **Metrics:** review minutes, critical/minor corrections, missing evidence, disagreement, confidence,
  and whether the packet is suitable for sign-off.
- **Pass:** ≥25% median time reduction with no increase in critical error and complete traceability.
- **Disagreement:** adjudicate and preserve both initial labels; do not train on unresolved consensus.

## Outcome study

Application outcomes are observational and affected by selection, labour market, candidate quality and
employer behaviour. Measure progression, but do not claim causality from before/after conversion alone.

- Minimum events: recommendation viewed, reason expanded, evidence corrected, decision confirmed,
  applied/skipped/review requested, interview, offer/rejection/withdrawal and user-reported reason.
- Preserve the decision and rule version that preceded the outcome.
- Compare similar cohorts and publish uncertainty.
- “More interviews” remains prohibited until a sufficiently powered design supports it.

## Session evidence record

Each session stores participant code, recruitment source, consent version, target-segment criteria,
scenario IDs, condition order, timestamps, answers, behavioural decisions, researcher notes,
recording/transcript location, incentive, withdrawals and analysis exclusions. Direct identifiers are
stored separately with restricted access.

## External sign-off protocol

For each production route, provide the reviewer:

- exact atomic rules and source revisions;
- assumptions and output wording;
- golden, boundary and adversarial cases;
- known contradictions and product abstention behaviour;
- change-monitoring/replay process;
- requested scope: factual correctness, omissions, advice boundary and permitted user outputs.

The signed record must name scope, limitations, qualifications/authority basis, effective bundle,
signature date, expiry/review trigger and conflicts. A paid invoice or generic consultation does not
constitute sign-off.

## Decision thresholds

| Decision        | Continue                              | Pause                                   | Stop/pivot                                |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Problem         | Repeated evidence from target users   | Mixed/segment-specific                  | Generic job-search pain dominates         |
| Trust           | Comprehension and flaw detection pass | Fixable wording/UX gaps                 | Users systematically over-rely            |
| Behaviour       | Errors decrease versus baselines      | No clear difference                     | False-green or harmful decisions increase |
| Payment         | Real payments and repeat use          | Interest without repeat                 | No payment after qualified exposure       |
| Economics       | Contribution improves with volume     | Review/support too costly but reducible | Delivery cost structurally exceeds price  |
| Expert workflow | Faster with equal/better safety       | No time gain                            | More critical errors or unusable packets  |
| Data moat       | Corrections/outcomes improve system   | Data sparse/noisy                       | No consent-compatible proprietary signal  |

## Reporting rule

Report denominators, exclusions and negative findings. “Users loved it” is not evidence. No study is
complete until raw records, analysis queries, deviations and decision consequences are stored.
