# European Technology-Mobility Country Dossiers

**Coverage:** 20 markets  
**Status:** R1 desk-research portfolio. No dossier is legal advice or a production rule bundle.

The dossiers identify route shape, decision inputs, machine-checkable opportunities and blocking
evidence. Source IDs resolve to `source-ledger.csv`. Numeric values are repeated only where current
2026 national evidence has been captured; older values are labelled stale.

## Portfolio-wide conclusion

R1 coverage across 20 markets establishes research breadth, not reliability. Wave placement remains a
portfolio hypothesis. Promotion requires current national sources, rule fixtures, adversarial cases,
expert sign-off, output permission, monitoring/replay and a commercial reason to bear ongoing cost.
