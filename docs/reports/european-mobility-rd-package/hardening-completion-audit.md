## High-value findings

1. **Eligibility checking is not the moat.** Mobility incumbents already offer it with expert
   fulfilment. AutoTime must prove vacancy-level decision quality, inspectability and candidate-owned
   evidence.
2. **Route separation is foundational.** Netherlands HSM and Blue Card use different employer and
   qualification logic even when some 2026 salary values coincide. German IT routes contain
   easily-confused experience conditions.
3. **A sponsor register has narrow semantics.** It proves a dated legal recognition assertion for a
   matched entity—not vacancy willingness, group-company status or application success.
4. **Historical replay is customer value, not merely audit infrastructure.** It explains why a prior
   decision changed when thresholds, lists, sources or evidence changed.
5. **The safest engine is selective.** Unknown/conflict/stale states are correct outputs; market count
   and answer rate are dangerous north stars.
6. **The moat is an operating system.** Source maintenance, expert labels, corrections, entity
   resolution and outcomes must lower error or delivery cost over time. Features alone do not compound.
7. **Regulation affects product shape.** Candidate-specific course-of-action output may cross an
   immigration-advice boundary; employer-facing ranking would materially change AI/employment risk.
8. **Expansion is an economics decision after safety.** A country should not launch if source and
   expert continuity cannot be funded by observed corridor contribution.

## Remaining gates

The following remain genuinely incomplete: primary-law reconciliation beyond the strengthened
beachhead chain; executable and expert-labelled cases; qualified sign-off; DPIA and written legal
opinions; founder-led UAT; real payments/retention; competitive product execution; production service
verification; retention/deletion/recovery drills; and observed PMF/moat evidence.

These are not document-writing gaps. Their protocols, records and pass/fail consequences are now
specified so they cannot be replaced by confidence or simulation.
