# Founder Investment Memo

## Recommendation

Fund a narrow Germany–Netherlands **evidence-backed application decision system**, not a broad
European job-application suite. Hold France, Spain, Ireland and Sweden as research options. Do not
fund 20-market production coverage until the first corridor proves comprehension, behavioural value,
payment, repeat use and maintainable delivery economics.

This recommendation is conditional. It should be reversed if the falsification programme shows that
official checklists or general LLMs produce equivalent decisions, users do not value provenance, or
expert/source operations remain structurally uneconomic.

## The strategic insight

The market gap is not “people cannot find visa information.” Official portals provide information and
mobility incumbents already offer eligibility checks with expert support. The candidate's difficult
decision happens earlier and combines four uncertain objects:

1. Is this exact vacancy worth the application effort?
2. Is the employing legal entity observable and relevant to sponsorship?
3. Which route-specific facts are supported, missing or contradictory?
4. Which candidate/application claims are backed by reusable evidence?

AutoTime can win if it makes this combined decision more correct, inspectable and reusable than the
alternatives. A generic visa answer, résumé score or country badge will be copied or bundled.

## Capital and time allocation

Until the first investment gate, allocate scarce product effort approximately:

- **35–45%:** canonical evidence, rule, decision, replay and correction platform;
- **20–25%:** Germany/Netherlands source operations and expert-labelled cases;
- **15–20%:** target-user research, paid concierge delivery and cohort analytics;
- **10–15%:** regulatory/privacy/security/production controls;
- **no more than 10%:** non-core workflow polish needed to deliver the experiment.

These are allocation guardrails, not accounting forecasts. Change them when observed bottlenecks and
unit costs exist.

## Product packaging sequence

### Offer 1 — Decision Pack

Five real DE/NL vacancy assessments, evidence gaps, narrow employer-register checks and reusable
candidate facts. Human/manual involvement is disclosed. Its purpose is to test whether the decision
is valuable before subscription infrastructure becomes the strategy.

### Offer 2 — Evidence Workspace

A recurring workspace for users who make multiple applications: current candidate facts, decision
history, source changes, corrections and claim controls. Launch only if Decision Pack users return for
multiple vacancies.

### Offer 3 — Expert-Reviewed Corridor

A structured packet reviewed within an authorised, documented scope. Launch only if regulation,
reviewer authority, response time and contribution margin support it.

Do not lead with “career platform,” unlimited AI writing or pan-European coverage. Those promises
increase surface area before the differentiated outcome is proven.

## Investment gates

| Gate          | Evidence needed                                              | Continue                                  | Narrow/stop                                     |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Problem       | 8–12 recent-decision interviews with artifacts               | Repeated pre-application pain dominates   | Generic discovery/CV pain dominates             |
| Reliability   | Signed corpus and zero critical false-green release failures | Safe selective outputs                    | Persistent unsafe certainty                     |
| Trust         | Blinded comprehension/reliance study                         | Users understand reasons/limits           | Users systematically over-rely                  |
| Behaviour     | Comparison with unaided and official-checklist baselines     | Fewer inappropriate decisions             | No advantage or worse false positives           |
| Payment       | Real checkout and delivered paid cohort                      | At least initial paid/repeat threshold    | Interest without payment/repeat                 |
| Economics     | Receipts reconciled to all variable delivery costs           | Contribution trend supports scale         | Expert/source burden structurally exceeds price |
| Defensibility | Same-scenario competitor benchmark                           | Inspectability/outcome advantage persists | General LLM/checklist matches result            |

No single gate substitutes for another. Payments do not establish safety; accuracy does not establish
demand.

## Twelve-month board-level milestones

### 0–90 days

- Canonical DE/NL route/evidence architecture in shadow/private evaluation.
- Expert-labelled benchmark, source monitoring and sponsor-entity benchmark.
- Problem/comprehension studies and first real paid Decision Packs.
- Written advice-boundary/DPIA decisions and production-service verification.

Decision: proceed, narrow to evidence workspace/expert co-pilot, partner, or stop.

### 3–6 months

- Paid private cohort with repeat decisions and reconciled contribution.
- Demonstrated replay/correction/source-change incident.
- Same-scenario competitor results.
- One delivery model selected: software-only, reviewed or partner-led.

Decision: fund repeatability or avoid subscription/market expansion.

### 6–12 months

- Sustained DE/NL operating evidence across multiple cohorts.
- Only the strongest of France/Spain/Ireland/Sweden enters a gated build.
- Evidence that review/source cost improves with corpus growth.
- Claims and release status reviewed at board/founder level.

Decision: selective expansion, deeper corridor focus, B2B expert tooling or halt.

## Metrics that matter

North-star candidate: **qualified decisions that lead to an evidence-appropriate action**, not
applications sent. Supporting metrics:

- critical false-green rate;
- comprehension and flawed-output detection;
- missing-evidence resolution;
- repeat vacancy decisions and second payment;
- contribution per delivered decision;
- source change time-to-triage;
- expert minutes and corrections per case;
- confirmed employer-match precision;
- correction closure and affected-decision replay;
- user deletion/complaint/incident rates.

Applications, AI generations and countries listed are activity metrics and can reward unsafe breadth.

## Claims policy

Until supported, prohibit: “eligible,” “verified employer” without narrow assertion context, “legal
advice,” “more interviews,” “improves outcomes,” “all Europe,” “complete coverage,” and “always
current.” Prefer: “shows the official evidence behind a pre-application decision,” with route,
market, date and limitations visible.

## Founder decision today

Approve one focused thesis for the next tranche:

> AutoTime will test whether non-EU technology candidates pay repeatedly for safer, evidence-linked
> Germany/Netherlands vacancy decisions, and whether the evidence/review system improves faster than
> its delivery cost.

Everything not necessary to test that statement should be maintained, paused or removed from the
near-term roadmap.
