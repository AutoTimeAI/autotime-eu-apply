## Evidence levels

- **R0 — discovered:** at least one apparently authoritative source exists.
- **R1 — structured:** route, controlling facts, source dates and unresolved gaps are recorded.
- **R2 — internally verified:** primary law/guidance reconciled and boundary cases independently reviewed.
- **R3 — expert-signed:** qualified jurisdiction reviewer signs the exact rule bundle and output scope.
- **R4 — production-observed:** monitoring, replay, corrections, incidents and real outcomes meet SLOs.

Only R3 or R4 may be marketed as supported. R0–R2 may be used for research or explicitly labelled
private evaluation. External UAT and expert sign-off cannot be completed by software agents and must
never be represented as complete.

## Research rules

1. Record one ledger row per source/claim cluster; never cite a search-result page.
2. Prefer primary law and competent authority guidance. Vendor material supports competitor claims
   only, never legal conclusions.
3. Store route-level rules. A country-level salary threshold is invalid when parallel routes differ.
4. Record `retrieved_at`, `published_or_updated_at`, `effective_from`, language and supersession.
5. A current page with stale embedded values is a contradiction, not permission to guess.
6. Unknown and review-required are correct outcomes. Missing evidence never becomes a pass.
7. Every release claim must distinguish desk research, internal verification, user evidence and
   external professional sign-off.

## Completion boundary

The package is not complete merely when all files exist. Completion requires source-level coverage,
route rules, counterexamples, commercial evidence and external sign-off for every market that will be
marketed. Countries may remain research-only without blocking a completed strategic portfolio, but
their status and omissions must remain explicit.
