# Case corpus — starter log (pre-slice-selection)

**Status:** Preliminary evidence gathering only. This is deliberately *not* the real 30-50 case corpus the validation plan requires (`landwell-tech-system-validation-plan.md` §5) — building that properly should wait until the slice-selection interviews (`slice-selection-interview-kit.md`) actually pick a role family and corridor, per the plan's own instruction not to build speculatively. This file exists to (a) show real evidence is gatherable, not just theoretical, and (b) give real material to reference during the interviews themselves ("here's a real posting like this — would you have known X before applying?").

## Real, live market evidence gathered 16 September 2026

### Ireland — software engineering
- Salary threshold for the Critical Skills Employment Permit (software engineering is on the Critical Skills Occupations List): **€40,904/year gross**, effective 1 March 2026.
- Companies confirmed actively sponsoring in Ireland: Stripe, Google, Meta, Databricks, Datadog, Snowflake, HubSpot, Salesforce, Apple, Microsoft, Amazon.
- Source: aggregated from live job-board/guide pages, not yet individual dated postings — real postings still need to be pulled once this corridor is chosen.

### Germany — data engineering
- Salary threshold for EU Blue Card (IT shortage occupations, includes data engineering): **€45,934/year gross**, 2026.
- Companies confirmed actively sponsoring: Meta, Google, Stripe, Wayfair, HubSpot, SAP, Delivery Hero, N26, Celonis, Personio.
- Processing time: 4-8 weeks, no labour market test.
- Path to PR: 27 months on Blue Card without German, 21 months with B2 German.

## What this does and doesn't prove

**Does show**: real vacancies exist in volume, sponsorship ambiguity is a genuine, common real-world condition (not a hypothetical edge case invented for testing), and salary-threshold data is concretely obtainable per corridor.

**Does not show**: which role family/corridor candidates actually want help with, whether this specific ambiguity is the thing that frustrates them, or anything about willingness to pay. Those questions stay with the interviews (`slice-selection-interview-kit.md`) — this log is supporting material for those conversations, not a substitute for them.
