## What this ledger establishes

It is feasible to find varied **posting-language** cases on direct employer pages: explicit no, qualified positive, company-level some-role support, silence, eligibility wording, role-family mismatch, and inadequate description. The corpus still lacks enough **independent employers and clean mid/senior backend cases** to claim representative decision accuracy. Multiple Fin, Anthropic, and Dun & Bradstreet roles must not inflate employer diversity.

Before calling this a frozen ten-case evaluation set, capture immutable/dated vacancy evidence, verify employing entity and location, use consented or synthetic candidate profiles, obtain independent role labels and qualified mobility review for legal conclusions, then run the actual LandWell UI. Do not assign expected Apply/Skip outcomes from posting language alone: the candidate's evidenced skills and work rights are part of the decision unit.
