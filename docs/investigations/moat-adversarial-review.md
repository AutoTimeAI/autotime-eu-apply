# Adversarial moat review

## Verdict

AutoTime does not yet possess a proven moat. It has built prerequisites for a potentially defensible **decision-evidence learning system**. The strongest wedge is vacancy-specific, candidate-controlled cross-border application decisions with replayable provenance—not CV generation, autofill, tracking, country content or a static rules database.

## Replication attack

| Asset | Cheapest competitor response | Replication resistance now | What raises resistance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Country rules/citations | Buy legal content or scrape official sources | Low | Temporal source graph plus operational change history and quality record |
| Expert sign-offs | Retain immigration professionals | Low–medium | Fast scoped re-review workflow connected to affected decisions |
| Employer registers | Ingest public lists | Low | Entity resolution history plus vacancy-specific willingness evidence |
| Decision engine | Rebuild rules/LLM prompts | Medium | Tested bundles, edge-case corpus, correction/replay history |
| Candidate evidence | Ask users for the same facts | Low | Consented longitudinal evidence with correction quality and portability |
| Decision-to-outcome graph | Requires usage, consent, time and reliable attribution | Potentially high | Sufficient density, feedback quality, privacy governance and demonstrated lift |

## Failure modes

The moat thesis fails if users will not provide evidence; outputs do not change decisions; experts make operations uneconomic; rule maintenance costs grow faster than revenue; corrections do not improve future performance; public/competitor data delivers equivalent decisions; or privacy constraints prevent lawful compounding.

## Strategic choices

1. Concentrate on DE/NL and earn measurable trust before country expansion.
2. Treat explanations, disagreement and correction as core product surfaces, not compliance extras.
3. Instrument the chain from evidence → recommendation → action → verified outcome with consent and deletion support.
4. Publish calibrated limitations; reliability is the brand promise.
5. Buy commodity content/infrastructure; build the evidence graph, evaluation corpus and learning loop.

## Moat threshold

CID will call the moat “demonstrated” only when repeated cohorts show the governed system materially improves decision quality or efficiency, outcome-linked data improves later recommendations, economics remain positive after source/expert/support cost, and a credible competitor replication analysis shows meaningful time/data/operational disadvantage.
