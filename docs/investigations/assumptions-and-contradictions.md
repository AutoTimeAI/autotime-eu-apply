# Assumptions and contradictions

| CID ID | Assumption or contradiction | Current judgement | Disproof evidence / resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| CID-2026-015 | “Twenty researched markets means Europe is covered.” | False. Research coverage is not route release readiness. | Every market independently clears source, evaluation, expert, user and economics gates. |
| CID-2026-016 | “Immutable records automatically create trust.” | Unproved. Users may not understand provenance, and bad inputs can be immutably wrong. | Task-based comprehension, calibrated trust and correction studies. |
| CID-2026-017 | “Sponsor-register membership proves this vacancy sponsors.” | False. Entity membership and vacancy willingness are different claims. | Vacancy-specific statement plus employer/entity resolution. |
| CID-2026-018 | “More country breadth increases defensibility.” | Often false early: breadth multiplies maintenance cost and liability. | Positive contribution margin and accuracy SLA per additional market. |
| CID-2026-019 | “Germany and Netherlands are equivalent launch markets.” | False. Germany has revealed volume; Netherlands has strong register verifiability but adverse national-route trend. | Corridor-specific acquisition, decision-quality and paid-conversion data. |
| CID-2026-020 | “Rules/citations/expert reviews alone are a moat.” | False. Incumbents possess these assets. | Evidence that the proprietary feedback loop improves accuracy/value with scale. |
| CID-2026-021 | “Implemented migration equals production control.” | False. No production application evidence inspected. | Environment-bound migration and runtime proof. |
| CID-2026-022 | “A single mobility score is helpful.” | Risky. It can hide authority dependencies and mix job fit with legal evidence. | Users accurately explain reason codes and make safer decisions than control. |
| CID-2026-023 | “More interviews” is supportable marketing. | Not earned. No causal outcome evidence. | Preregistered outcome study with sufficient cohort and confound controls. |
| CID-2026-024 | “Expert sign-off remains valid until manually revisited.” | Unsafe. Sources and scope change. | Version-scoped expiry/withdrawal plus automatic demotion drill. |

Open country contradictions remain governed findings, not documentation defects: France salary sources conflict; Portugal, Italy, Belgium, Luxembourg and Estonia retain specified evidence gaps; Austria requires authority outcome; Switzerland is authority-review; UK scope requires an IAA opinion.
