# Private beta testing guide

For any AutoTime AI private-beta tester. Written for someone using the
product for the first time — no assumed technical background. Page names and
button labels below are taken directly from the current codebase
(`apps/web`), not guessed; see
[`README.md`](README.md#verified-facts-this-pack-relies-on) for the exact
file references.

This is informal testing to help us improve the product before public
launch. There's no "wrong" way to do it — if something confuses you, that's
useful information, not a mistake on your part.

## Before you start

- You do **not** need to submit a real job application anywhere. AutoTime
  never sends an application on your behalf — the furthest any task goes is
  reaching a "ready" state inside the app.
- Feel free to use placeholder contact details (name, email, phone) if you'd
  rather not enter real personal data while testing.
- **Country and sponsorship guidance is informational only.** It does not
  guarantee that you're eligible to work in a country, and it does not
  guarantee an employer will offer sponsorship. Always check official
  government/immigration sources and the employer directly before relying on
  anything the app tells you.

## Task 1 — Sign in and set up your profile

1. Go to the sign-in page and sign in with Google or GitHub.
2. If you see a waitlist screen, enter the invite code you were given, then
   continue to your dashboard.
3. On first visit you'll see the setup wizard (**Onboarding**). Work through
   it: add your background (CV/experience), the countries you're interested
   in working in, and your work-authorisation situation for those countries.

**Try**: leaving a field blank or going back a step, to see whether the
wizard handles that sensibly.

**Record**: anything that felt unclear, any step that seemed to ask for
information you didn't expect, and whether you understood why each question
was being asked.

## Task 2 — Look at three job opportunities

1. Go to the **Jobs** page.
2. Find or add three jobs relevant to you. For each one you can:
   - **Save job** — keeps it in your list.
   - **Analyse job** (or "Analyse this vacancy") — runs the fit/suitability
     check against your profile.
3. Read the result for each job.

**Try**: at least one job that's a strong match for your background, and one
that probably isn't, to see if the results feel sensible in both directions.

**Record**: whether the recommendation matched your own judgement, whether
the reasoning made sense, and anything that felt off or overconfident.

## Task 3 — Complete one application-readiness journey

1. Pick one of your analysed jobs and select **Prepare application**.
2. Go to the **Applications** page and open that application.
3. Work through the stages as far as you're willing to:
   - **Start final review**
   - **Mark ready** (only available once the app's "Application readiness"
     checklist is clear)
4. You can stop there. You do **not** need to click "Mark as applied" —
   that step is only for people about to submit a real application outside
   AutoTime, and it asks for explicit confirmation before doing anything.

**Try**: reading the "Application readiness" checklist if any items are
still open — see whether it's clear what's missing and why.

**Record**: how long it took to feel ready, whether the content generated
(if any) felt usable, and anything that blocked you unexpectedly.

## Task 4 — Explore country and sponsorship guidance

1. Go to the **Countries** area of the dashboard.
2. Look at the guidance for one or two countries you're targeting: work
   permission, sponsorship, and relocation information.

**Remember**: this is guidance, not a guarantee. It's meant to help you
understand the landscape, not replace official sources or an immigration
adviser.

**Record**: whether the information felt trustworthy and easy to follow,
and whether it was clear that it's guidance rather than a promise.

## Task 5 — Tell us what you think

Use the [feedback template](feedback-template.md) as a guide, or just write
freely. There is no working in-app feedback form yet, so please **reply to
the email you received** with your thoughts.

## Simple checklist

- [ ] Signed in and completed onboarding
- [ ] Looked at 3+ relevant job opportunities
- [ ] Completed one application-readiness journey (through "Mark ready" is
      enough — no real application needed)
- [ ] Looked at country/sponsorship guidance for at least one country
- [ ] Sent feedback by replying to the welcome email

## Troubleshooting and contact

- Something broken, confusing, or unclear at any point? Reply to your
  welcome email — include roughly what you were doing and what you expected
  to happen, no need for technical detail.
- If you take a screenshot to show us something, please blank out or crop
  any personal information first (name, email, phone, CV contents, etc.).
- This is a beta: things may be rough in places, and we don't guarantee job,
  interview, visa, or sponsorship outcomes. That's exactly what we're trying
  to learn from your feedback.
