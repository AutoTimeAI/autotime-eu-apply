**What we'd love you to try, over the next 7 days**

No pressure on all of it — even partial testing is genuinely useful — but
here's what would help most:

- Complete the setup steps and build an accurate profile (your real
  background is fine, but feel free to use placeholder contact details if
  you'd rather not enter real personal data during testing).
- Look at **at least three job opportunities** that are actually relevant to
  you.
- Go through **one full application-readiness journey** for a job you're
  interested in — right up to the point where the app says it's ready. You
  do not need to submit a real job application anywhere; AutoTime never
  submits applications on your behalf, so there's nothing to undo if you stop
  before that point.
- Have a look at the country/sponsorship guidance area — it's there to help
  you understand work-permission and sponsorship considerations for
  different countries.
- Tell us honestly what you think, including anything that felt confusing,
  broken, or just not useful.

We're especially interested to know: **which roles and which countries are
you targeting?** That helps us understand whether what you're seeing is
actually relevant to your search.
