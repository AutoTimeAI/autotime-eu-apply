# UAT session guide — 3–5 person usability test

Use this for a human-facilitated session of about 30 minutes per participant. Do not automate it or count an internal checklist run as UAT.

## Preparation

- Recruit 3–5 EU-focused job seekers, including at least one or two non-technical participants.
- Create a fresh account for every participant.
- Record the screen only with explicit consent.
- Say: “I’m testing the product, not you. There are no wrong answers. Please think aloud.”

## Tasks

Give one task at a time. Do not reveal later tasks in advance.

1. “Sign up and set up your profile as if you were really using this to find a job.”
2. “Find a role you’d genuinely be interested in.”
3. “Look at your match score for that role and tell me what you think it means.”
4. “Get your CV ready to apply for that role.”
5. “Try reaching out to someone at the company about this role.” Then ask: “If you click Copy, is anything sent automatically?”
6. If the extension is in scope: “Use the browser extension on a job posting.” Ask the participant to explain the LinkedIn disclosure afterward.

Do not help unless the participant is stuck for at least 60 seconds or gives up. When asked what something means, first ask what they think it means and record that interpretation.

## Triage

- Treat friction repeated by at least 3 of 5 participants as a product signal.
- Treat any uncertainty about whether outreach is automatically sent as a release-blocking copy/interaction problem.
- Separate launch blockers from post-beta polish in the consolidated report.
