## Goal

Create a polished first-time-user demo that shows AutoTime EU Apply as a serious job-search operating system for UK/EU applications: profile evidence, role fit, AI assistance, extension workflow, application tracking, interview prep, and Pro upgrade.

## Full Voiceover Script

Applying across UK and EU roles gets messy fast.

You are comparing job descriptions, checking sponsorship and location risk, rewriting answers, tracking follow-ups, and preparing for interviews, usually across five different tools.

AutoTime EU Apply brings that workflow into one AI-assisted workspace.

First, you create a reusable candidate profile. This is not just a form. It is the evidence base the product uses: target roles, work-right details, CV text, project examples, locations, and facts the AI is allowed to use.

When you find a role, open Job Check. Add the title, company, URL, location, and role description. AutoTime turns the job into a structured fit review.

The verdict is designed to be transparent. You see a fit score, the recommendation, why it was given, what evidence is missing, and which risks still need manual confirmation. It helps you decide whether to apply, tailor, pause, or skip.

The Chrome extension brings this workflow onto supported ATS platforms like Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, Ashby, SmartRecruiters, iCIMS, BambooHR, Teamtailor, Recruitee, Jobvite, and Personio.

On LinkedIn, AutoTime stays conservative. It uses manual copy and paste only, so the user remains in control.

Once the profile and role are ready, AutoTime can draft application content from your real evidence: a cover letter, motivation answer, strengths answer, availability answer, and a concise profile summary.

Every role can be saved into the application tracker with status, notes, source, next action, and follow-up date. That makes the search easier to manage across weeks, not just one application at a time.

When a role progresses, Interview Prep creates a role-specific pack with likely questions, answer structure, risks to prepare for, and questions to ask the employer.

Free is enough to start. Pro is for serious search volume: cloud sync, unlimited AI job analysis, AI application content, and interview prep.

AutoTime EU Apply is not a magic apply button. It is a serious copilot for making better, faster, evidence-backed job search decisions.
